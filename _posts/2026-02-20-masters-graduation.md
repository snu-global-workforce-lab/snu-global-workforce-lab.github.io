We are delighted to celebrate the graduation of two master's students from the **SNU Global Workforce Development Lab** in **February 2026**.

**Jiyeon Kim** and **Jihyeon An** have successfully completed their master's studies and are beginning exciting new chapters in their careers.

- **Jihyeon An** will start her new role as an **Admissions Officer at Konkuk University**.
- **Jiyeon Kim** is preparing to pursue **Ph.D. studies abroad**.

We warmly congratulate both of them on this important milestone and wish them great success in their future endeavors.

The lab is proud of their achievements and looks forward to their continued contributions to education and workforce development.
